Your privacy

What we collect, why, and who sees it. Last updated October 3, 2026.

What we collect

If you request a free assessment, you give us your name, role, email and phone number, and how and when you'd like us to reach you. You also tell us your business name, type, city and service area, links and details for your website and online profiles, your goals, budget and timeline, and any notes you add.

The scorecard on the home page and the quiz don't send anything to us on their own. If you go on to the assessment, your quiz answers travel with you in the page address and are filled into the notes field.

How your request reaches us

When you submit the assessment form, your answers are emailed to us. This website does not keep a copy.

How we use it

We use it to prepare your assessment, reply to you and, if you choose to work with us, set up your project. We don't sell your information.

Who else is involved

This site is hosted on Cloudflare, which handles normal web traffic data, such as your IP address, to deliver pages. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google also receives your IP address when a page loads. Your email provider and ours carry the message you send. We don't run analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

Your choices

Email us any time to see, correct or delete what you've sent us.
